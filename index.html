1976 South Asia

Jobra Village, Bangladesh

The birthplace of microcredit. Professor Yunus made a personal loan of $27 to 42 stool-makers in Jobra village near Chittagong University, demonstrating that poor entrepreneurs are bankable.

Primary Initiative: Jobra Experimental Project
Impact Scale: Ground Zero ($27 Loan)
Archive Reference: DOC-BD-1976

Historical Journey

Chronology of Transformation

Follow the evolutionary milestones of Professor Yunus from his academic roots to world-changing economic paradigms.

1976 • CHITTAGONG

The Jobra Micro-Lending Experiment

Faced with devastating regional famine, Economics Professor Yunus steps outside theoretical models and lends $27 out of his own pocket to 42 local artisans trapped by predatory moneylenders.

TAG: ARCHIVE-ORIGINS
TAG: BANKING-REVOLUTION
1983 • BANGLADESH

Grameen Bank Formalized

Grameen Bank is established by special national ordinance. Reversing conventional banking rules requiring collateral, Grameen relies on mutual trust, peer accountability groups, and female borrowers.

2006 • OSLO, NORWAY

Nobel Peace Prize Awarded

Muhammad Yunus and Grameen Bank are jointly awarded the Nobel Peace Prize "for their efforts to create economic and social development from below."

TAG: NOBEL-LAUREATE
TAG: GLOBAL-MOVEMENT
2007–2020 • GLOBAL

The 3-Zero Social Business Model

Yunus formulates the framework of non-dividend Social Business aiming for a world of **Three Zeros**: Zero Net Carbon Emission, Zero Wealth Concentration, and Zero Unemployment.

2024 • DHAKA

Chief Adviser, Interim Government

Following historic student-led uprisings in August 2024, Professor Yunus assumes leadership of Bangladesh's Interim Government to oversee institutional reforms and democratic renewal.
